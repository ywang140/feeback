01 / Privacy

Privacy隐私政策

生效日期 2026-11-23

一句话：FeeBack 不收集你的任何数据。你记的东西只存在你自己的 iPhone 里，我们看不到。

数据存在哪

你添加的卡、开卡月份、每项额度用没用、提醒和语言设置，都只保存在这台 iPhone 上 FeeBack 自己的存储空间里。FeeBack 没有账号，也没有服务器，这些内容不会上传到任何地方。

不连银行，不要卡号

FeeBack 不会、也不需要连接你的银行或信用卡账户，不会让你填卡号、账号、密码或任何验证码。所有记录都是你自己手动点的。

不联网，不追踪

  • App 不向任何服务器发送数据。
  • 没有广告，没有统计、分析或追踪工具，也没有任何第三方 SDK。
  • 不会跨 App 或网站追踪你。

用到的权限

  • 通知：用来在额度快过期时提醒你。提醒是手机在本地生成的，不经过服务器。你可以随时在 iPhone 设置里关掉。
  • 照片（仅添加）：只有你点「保存」时，才会把年度汇总图存进相册。FeeBack 不会读取、浏览或上传你的照片。

你主动分享或导出的内容

你可以用系统的分享菜单发出年度汇总图，或者导出 CSV。东西发到哪里是你自己选的，发出去之后由你选的那个 App 或服务按它的规则处理。

备份

如果你开了 iCloud 云备份，或者用电脑备份 iPhone，系统可能会把 FeeBack 的数据一起备份。这部分由 Apple 按它自己的隐私政策处理，我们同样看不到。

删除数据

在 FeeBack 的「设置 → 清空所有记录」里可以一键删除全部记录。删除 App 也会把它在手机上的数据一起删掉。

儿童

FeeBack 不收集任何人的个人信息，包括 13 岁以下的儿童。

政策更新

如果这份政策有改动，我们会更新这个页面和上面的生效日期。以后如果 App 开始收集任何数据，会先在这里写清楚，再在 App 里告诉你。

PrivacyPrivacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-23

In one line: FeeBack collects no data at all. What you log stays on your own iPhone, and we can’t see it.

Where your data lives

The cards you add, when you opened them, which credits you’ve used, and your reminder and language settings are stored only in FeeBack’s own storage on this iPhone. FeeBack has no accounts and no servers, so none of it is uploaded anywhere.

No bank connection, no card numbers

FeeBack doesn’t connect to your bank or credit card accounts and never asks for card numbers, account numbers, passwords or verification codes. Everything is logged by you, by hand.

No network, no tracking

  • The app sends no data to any server.
  • No ads, no analytics or tracking tools, no third-party SDKs.
  • FeeBack does not track you across other apps or websites.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to remind you before a credit expires. Reminders are scheduled on your phone, not sent from a server. You can turn them off any time in iPhone Settings.
  • Photos (add only): used only when you tap Save to put your year summary image in your library. FeeBack never reads, browses or uploads your photos.

What you choose to share or export

You can send your year summary image or export a CSV through the system share sheet. You choose where it goes, and from then on that app or service handles it under its own terms.

Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, your FeeBack data may be included. Apple handles those backups under its own privacy policy, and we can’t see them either.

Deleting your data

Settings → Erase all records in FeeBack deletes everything at once. Deleting the app also removes its data from your phone.

Children

FeeBack collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the effective date above. If FeeBack ever starts collecting any data, we’ll say so here first and tell you in the app.

02 / Support

Support使用帮助

遇到问题先看看下面，没找到答案就直接发邮件给我。

FeeBack 是干嘛的？

一张美国信用卡的 checklist。每项额度是一个泡泡，用了点一下就记上；快过期的会提醒你；首页一眼看到今年拿回了多少、年费回本没有。

换手机或者删了 App，记录还在吗？

记录只存在手机里。换新 iPhone 时用 iCloud 备份恢复或者「快速开始」迁移，一般会一起带过去。删掉 App 记录就没了，建议先在「设置 → 导出数据」导出一份 CSV 留档（目前 CSV 还不能导回 App）。

某张卡的额度或金额不对？

发卡行经常调整权益。发邮件告诉我卡名和哪一项不对，我会在下一个版本更新。在那之前，你可以在额度里自己调用掉的金额。

收不到到期提醒？

先看 iPhone「设置 → 通知 → FeeBack」有没有允许通知，再看 FeeBack 里「设置 → 提醒」有没有打开。

同一张卡办了两张怎么记？

点开那张卡的卡面，选「我还有一张这样的卡」。会编号 01、02，各记各的。

想重看教程？

「设置 → 使用教程」。

FeeBack 和银行有关系吗？

没有。FeeBack 是独立开发的 App，与 American Express、Chase、Capital One、Citi 等发卡机构都没有关联。卡面是简化示意图，权益以发卡行的官方条款为准。

SupportHelp & FAQ

Check below first. If you don’t find your answer, just email me.

What is FeeBack?

A checklist for your US credit cards. Each credit is a bubble you tap when you’ve used it. You get reminded before credits expire, and Home shows how much you’ve got back this year and whether each annual fee has paid for itself.

Will my records survive a new phone or deleting the app?

Records live on your phone. Moving to a new iPhone with an iCloud backup or Quick Start usually brings them along. Deleting the app removes them, so export a CSV first in Settings → Export data (CSV can’t be imported back yet).

A card’s credit or amount is wrong?

Issuers change benefits often. Email me the card name and which credit is off, and I’ll fix it in the next update. Until then you can adjust the amount you used inside the credit.

Not getting reminders?

Check iPhone Settings → Notifications → FeeBack, then Settings → Reminders inside FeeBack.

Two of the same card?

Open the card face and choose “I have another one of these”. They’re numbered 01 and 02 and tracked separately.

See the tutorial again?

Settings → How it works.

Is FeeBack affiliated with any bank?

No. FeeBack is an independent app, not affiliated with American Express, Chase, Capital One, Citi or any other issuer. Card faces are simplified illustrations; the issuer’s official terms always win.
